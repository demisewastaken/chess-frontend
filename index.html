Chess Rulebook

Learn the rules of chess, how each piece moves and captures, special moves, and the main concepts to help you play and enjoy the game.

1

The Pieces

Chess is played with six different types of pieces. Each piece moves in a unique way.

King

Moves one square in any direction.

Capture: Replaces enemy piece 1 square away.
Special: Cannot move into check. Castles with Rook.

Queen

Moves any number of squares in any direction.

Capture: Takes first enemy piece along line.
Special: Combines Rook and Bishop. Cannot jump.

Rook

Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically.

Capture: Takes first enemy piece along rank or file.
Special: Participates in Castling with King.

Bishop

Moves any number of squares diagonally.

Capture: Takes first enemy piece along diagonal.
Special: Always remains on its starting color.

Knight

Moves in an L-shape and can jump over other pieces.

Capture: Takes piece on its landing square.
Special: The only piece that leaps over others.

Pawn

Moves forward, captures diagonally, and has special rules.

Capture: 1 square diagonally forward.
Special: Promotes on 8th rank. En Passant capable.
2

The Objective & Fundamentals

Objective of the Game

Chess is played between two opponents on an 8×8 board of 64 alternating squares. White moves first, followed by Black. The primary objective is to checkmate the enemy King.

Check vs. Checkmate

  • Check: The King is directly under attack. The player must escape check immediately on that turn.
  • Checkmate: The King is in check and has no legal move to escape. The game ends immediately in victory for the attacker.

The Three Escapes from Check (CPR)

  • Capture: Capture the attacking enemy piece.
  • Protect: Block the line of attack by placing a piece between the checker and King.
  • Run: Move the King to an unattacked square.

King Safety Restriction

A player can never make a move that exposes their own King to check or leaves their King in check. Pinned pieces cannot move off the pin line if doing so reveals check.

3

Special Moves

Castling (Kingside & Queenside)

A unique two-piece move providing King safety and Rook activation. The King moves two squares toward a Rook, and the Rook hops over to the adjacent square.

  • Kingside (O-O): King moves two squares right; Rook moves two squares left.
  • Queenside (O-O-O): King moves two squares left; Rook moves three squares right.
  • Conditions: Neither piece has moved prior; squares between them are empty; King is not in check, nor passes through or lands on attacked squares.

Pawn Promotion

When a pawn reaches the farthest rank (rank 8 for White, rank 1 for Black), it immediately transforms into a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.

Promotion is not limited to captured pieces; having multiple Queens is fully legal.

En Passant ("In Passing")

If an enemy pawn advances two squares on its initial move and lands directly adjacent to your pawn, you may capture it diagonally behind on your immediate next turn as if it had only moved one square.

Timing restriction: En passant must be played immediately on the next turn or the right is permanently lost.

4

Draws & Endgame

Stalemate

Occurs when the player whose turn it is has no legal moves available, but their King is not in check. The game immediately ends as a draw (½ - ½).

50-Move Rule & Threefold Repetition

  • 50-Move Rule: If 50 consecutive moves occur with no pawn move and no piece capture, either player can claim a draw.
  • Threefold Repetition: If the exact same board position occurs three times, the game is an automatic draw.

Insufficient Material

When neither side has enough pieces to legally force checkmate (e.g., King vs. King, King + Bishop vs. King, or King + Knight vs. King), the match is immediately drawn.

Match Clocks & Inactivity Abort

  • 10-Minute Clocks: Each player starts with 10:00. Time counts down only on your turn. Reaching 0:00 loses on time.
  • Inactivity Timer: If White makes no move within 30 seconds of match start, the game automatically aborts (with an official warning at 15 seconds).
5

Game Rules & Fair Play

Authoritative Move Validation

All moves are validated in real time by the backend engine. Illegal moves are rejected immediately, maintaining complete board integrity.

Resignation & Match Abort

Players may resign at any time during an active match. If an inactivity timeout occurs before the first move, the match is safely aborted.
